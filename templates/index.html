The Style Thesaurus let's you search automatically created thesauruses. Each thesaurus is generated using a large corpus of text, and words that appear in similar contexts are returned as 'synonyms'. It is not like a typical thesaurus; it eskews good synonyms for words that are related in some way. For instance, if you search for 'tender', in a typical thesuarus you might get something like this:

caring, affectionate, romantic, kindly, young

In contrast, this is the response from the Gandhi corpus:

fertile, soft, degraded, lovable, sturdy, dull

Few of these are precise synonyms, but rather give you some words around the word 'tender'. And this is the response from the Philosophy corpus:

passionate, humble, generous, bitter, gentle, cheerful

The exception is 'Oxford' thesaurus, which is a "normal" thesaurus: the Oxford American Writer's Thesaurus.

Is this powered by AI? Depends on what you mean by AI. These thesauruses are created by an automated analysis of text; a program was written to do this and the synonyms (or rather, nearby words) were not hand-selected. However, these thesauruses do not use language models of the sort currently powering popular AI tools, and they rely on relatively small (<10 megabytes) amounts of text.
